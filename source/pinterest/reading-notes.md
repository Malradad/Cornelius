## Patterns (on top of the portfolio and decks)
- **Drawing is the obsession.** 416 pins, more than all the interior boards combined.
- **The monumental frame.** A huge opening that frames landscape or sky. It is
  a threshold that turns the view into an exhibit (A Housing, Desert, studio).
- **Light through fabric.** A glowing tent or canopy appears on 3 boards
  (House N, Rustic, studio). A signature image he keeps saving.
- **Classical figures in modern rooms.** Statues and busts on the grid floor in
  the concept sketches; Canova in studio; a bust on a book cover in Portfolio.
- **One accent.** Editorial work in grey with one red; interiors in timber with
  one green or blue-grey. The same palette rule as the decks.
- **Regional, but surreal.** The arch in the desert, the tree through a frame:
  Saudi and Mediterranean references, staged like de Chirico.
