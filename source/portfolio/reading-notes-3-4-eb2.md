# Reading notes — Portfolio sections 3, 4, EB2

Status: DRAFT. Pending maker's corrections.
Covers: Unwinding Reveries (2021), XATA (2022), Palindrome (2021),
Eleutheromania (2020), Cinematic Rhythms (2020), Binary Library (2019),
and EB2: professional work at Lissoni New York (2023–24).

## New patterns across the whole portfolio
1. **He thinks in pairs.** Palindrome, Binary/Bipolar Library ("Two-Faced",
   plan vs. section), Input/Output, existing vs. graft, East vs. West tower,
   densification vs. decomposition, New York vs. Riyadh. Mirrors, reversals, doubles.
2. **Architecture as a psychological instrument.** Claustrophobia
   (Eleutheromania), the resting mind (Unwinding Reveries), generational trauma
   (Deconstruction Underway), "a psychological space" (Binary Library). The brief
   is often a state of mind, not a program.
3. **Time is a material.** "Slow space", "durational", "experience of time",
   cinema and narrative, process over product, past and present as layers.
4. **Boundary without enclosure.** Curtains, bands and sliding planes
   (Eleutheromania); peg and operable walls (XATA); the dense vertical screen
   that becomes a solid (Cinematic Rhythms). It is the same obsession as the
   boundary-as-carrier idea, at the scale of the room.
5. **Strange, erudite titles.** Eleutheromania, Palindrome, Unwinding Reveries,
   The Imaginary Variance. Titles are where his voice is most his own.

## Visual language, refined
- **Grey is the base state.** 2019–2021 work is nearly all monochrome: grey
  collage, fine line, dark cinematic renders. Color arrives around 2022 and
  only as intervention or code (XATA's cobalt and yellow; IV's palette).
- **Recurring collage props:** the moon, flocks of birds, the reflective sphere
  (Unwinding Reveries' solar orb, which reappears on the cover), mountains,
  trains. A quiet surrealism.
- **Models and prototypes:** white models; for XATA, real built plywood furniture.
- **Hand sketches** survive into professional work (Bucherer, Moorings, Pompano).

## EB2 — professional work (Lissoni)
- Lissoni house language: warm wood, fluted glass, green marble, red onyx,
  burgundy lacquer, controlled light. Well executed, but the firm's voice.
- Where Meshal is visible: concept narratives (Mayan order, Mexican baroque,
  two towers as beach vs. yacht club), moodboards, hand perspectives, and
  millwork and RCP sets.
- Voice: generic luxury copy. In ~20 pages: "luxurious" ×14, "immersive" ×11,
  "luxury" ×9, "sophisticated" ×8, "harmonious" ×8, "meticulously" ×7, "seamless(ly)" ×9.
