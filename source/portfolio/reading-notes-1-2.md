# Reading notes — Portfolio sections 1–2

Status: DRAFT. Pending maker's corrections.
Covers: cover, contents, Input/Output Correspondence (2024), The Imaginary
Variance (2023, with Halie Kim), Deconstruction Underway (2022, with Halie Kim).

## The through-line: the boundary is the carrier
- **I/O Correspondence:** information on a 2D boundary generates 3D volume,
  and the volume feeds back to rewrite the boundary. Lebbeus Woods is cited.
- **Imaginary Variance:** the facades and interiors of MoMA, NYSE and the Roxy
  are grafted with fragments of collective memory. The envelope is where the
  counter-narrative enters.
- **Topological Design Systems (CV):** "surface-based information encoding."
→ Guess #2 strengthened: surface or boundary as the carrier of information
  and memory, and as the site of intervention.

## Visual language
- **Grey given, colored graft.** The existing city is drawn as a fine grey line
  ghost; the intervention arrives as saturated collage. Color is the act of
  intervention.
- **Palette:** teal, terracotta red, ochre, cobalt, blush pink on white and grey.
  Color is also a coding system (MoMA red, NYSE teal, Roxy ochre).
- **Drawing types:** dense cutaway sections and axonometrics, populated with
  collaged figures and tagged fragments (A07, B15, C22).
- **Taxonomy grids:** 48 fragment studies; 15 pavilion typologies; 5-step
  process strips. He thinks in catalogs.
- **Hand + math:** projective-geometry sketches beside equations set in black panels.
- **Models:** all white, with small color tabs keyed to the drawings.
- **Cover:** surreal, with chrome spheres reflecting a horizon, slender pointed
  arches, and a giant name split ME / SHAL bleeding off the page.

## Typography and layout
- Bold grotesque caps titles ending in a period ("THE IMAGINARY VARIANCE.").
  The CV uses the same habit, so it is a signature.
- Italic subtitles; small grey running header "2023 | PROJECT"; a light grey
  vertical "RESEARCH WORK" label; vertical color bars carrying site names.
- Generous white margins, with text tucked into one lower-left block.

## Thinking and method
- Defamiliarization, fragment, graft, hack, reassemble, counter-program.
- Architecture as narrative: "narrative as the primary catalyst."
- Targets institutions of power and privatization: art, finance, luxury
  hospitality, gentrification.
- Process over product; incompletion as an invitation (Deconstruction Underway).
- Opens with questions: "What narratives can emerge through the
  defamiliarization of formal fragments…?" This is radical questioning on the page.

## Voice (research writing)
- Manifesto register: challenge, counteract, disrupt, democratize, reimagine.
- Strongest when it asks; weakest at the endings, which fall back on the same
  formula ("more inclusive, equitable…") on several spreads.
- Typos: "REVERIEES", "DINNING", "variablity"; "BIPOLAR LIBRARY" lacks the
  period the other titles have.

## Questions for the maker
1. Imaginary Variance and Deconstruction Underway were made with Halie Kim.
   Which moves, drawings and words are his?
2. The Roxy critique targets privatized luxury hospitality, and his practice
   is now palaces and luxury hospitality. How does he hold that tension?
3. On the cover: what are the arches and the spheres? What do 12 and 15 refer to?
4. Deconstruction Underway is greyscale and quieter. Is that a deliberate
   register, or a different hand?
