# Reading notes — Grad school writings (2024–2026)

Sources: statements.pdf (MIT personal statement + SMArchS and Media Lab research
statements, v1/v2), smarchs.pdf, mde.pdf (Harvard MDE short answers + statements),
topological-design-research.pdf (private notes, Nov–Dec 2025), and
graduate-statements.pdf (an 8-page draft with raw internal notes and self-critique).
Purpose (maker): "a deeper sense" of how he thinks. Context, not a brief.

## Two voices, again
- **The application voice** (2024): "push the boundaries", "the perfect place",
  "pioneers… new frontiers". The same 3 paragraphs reused across MIT SMArchS, the
  Media Lab, Harvard MDE and GSD. Interchangeable, institutional. The brochure
  register, in his own words this time.
- **The private voice** (notes and rants, 2025–26): sharp, aphoristic, his.
  - "I'm not curious, I'm epistemically compulsed."
  - "Curious is good, but it is also safe… You are only as curious as your morality allows."
  - "I would rather be legible than liked."
  - "The regret of invention is survivable. The regret of not knowing is not."
  - "Architecture should encode intelligence before it computes it."
  - "Spectacle is the problem, pragmatism is the pressure, information structure is
    the answer, and automation is the execution mechanism."
  - "Victims are defined by what happened; agency is defined by what you did next."
  - "My background did not give me perspective, it gave me a method: the ability to
    see the distance between how a system represents reality and what reality actually is."
- **His self-critique is his best writing.** His editor's notes on his own draft
  ("the difference between radical honesty and performing radical honesty"; "the essay
  ends right before it begins the actual story"; "zero external anchoring") are sharper
  than any finished statement.

## How his thinking moved
- 2024: autonomy, multiplicity, serendipity, error, quantum computing as a frame.
  One input → many outputs. Problem-setting over problem-solving (Donald Schön).
- 2025–26: discipline over spectacle. Computation is not intelligence; it executes
  intelligence. Encode the designer's hidden structure (hierarchies, dependencies,
  embedded expression) into relational systems "without flattening the sensibility
  that produced it." The goal: "the intelligence of a designer becomes transmissible."
- He admits being seduced by spectacle at Pratt ("I believed that my work needed
  to be visually spectacular to be relevant"). The image-maker distrusting the image,
  stated outright.
- **Unresolved oscillation:** private notes (Dec 2025) call for architectural
  autonomy, "geometrical first"; the final statement says "architecture is rarely
  autonomous." He hasn't settled where he stands.

## Correction to an earlier reading
- At Lissoni he built Python/Grasshopper relational systems for furniture, millwork,
  lighting and material: a change to one element recalibrates the others. The firm
  years were not only "the boring work"; he was running his research inside them.

## Rejects (in his words)
- Parametricism's "complexity, continuity, emergence" as proof of intelligence;
  "idiotic surface gestures"; "a newer romanticism of aesthetics"; topology reduced to
  Gehry/Zaha surfaces; computation as spectacle "in a different register".
- Identity performance and "adversity branding" in place of an actual idea.

## The raw draft: who he is underneath
- **Friction as engine.** "Interior pressure… friction… obsession, dissatisfaction,
  humiliation, and relentless self-critique." Growing up gay in Saudi Arabia was one
  of those frictions. He refuses to make it the story ("theatrical", "adversity
  branding"); he wants to be read by his ideas.
- **His resolution (his own notes):** not "context didn't shape me", but "these were
  the conditions I was handed, and here is what I chose to do inside them." Agency,
  not victimhood. Context gave him a method, not a perspective.
- **Truth over comfort.** "The question is no longer should this be known, but can
  this be known." He would rather create something with grave implications than
  live not knowing. He names the risk himself (god complex, narcissism) and refuses
  to sanitize it.
- **Allergic to performance,** including the performance of honesty.
- **His own verdict on that draft:** liability, no external anchoring, no payload;
  ground it in one concrete intellectual moment.
