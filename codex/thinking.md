# Thinking

## Confirmed by the maker
- **Visionary and image-maker first.** Meshal sees himself as a visionary driven
  by intellectual curiosity and radical questioning. Drawing and representation
  are his primary medium of thought. (Source: CV honors; maker's own words, 2026-11-17)
- **Two tracks, on purpose.** Professional practice (DD, CDs, CA, millwork) is
  the apprenticeship he is doing now. Research and authorship are the destination.
  He is in practice until his research and name can stand on their own.

## Working method
- Starts from a question, not a program. Radical questioning drives the work.
  (Still need examples of the actual questions.)
- **Between continuity and transformation.** The inherited condition (an
  existing building, a brand, a city) is a productive constraint. Keep what
  ages with dignity, isolate it, and present it as an artefact.
- **Allusion over quotation.** "A curve may recall a classical arch without
  becoming one."
- **The threshold or boundary is where the design happens.** It runs from the
  thesis facades to La Rustica's thresholds chapter.
- **Thinks in pairs:** rustic/pristine, old/new, opaque/translucent.

## Concept deck method (his standard, from solo work)
Cover with project number → a witty epigraph page → Introduction / Premise /
Content → I Context → II Proposal abstract (a 3–4 word principle set) →
III User or Origin → Design narrative (Aesthetics, Materials, Lighting,
Furniture, Sketches) → Summary → Glossary → Copyright.
