# Thinking

## Confirmed by the maker
- **Visionary and image-maker first.** Meshal sees himself as a visionary driven
  by intellectual curiosity and radical questioning. Drawing and representation
  are his primary medium of thought. (Source: CV honors; maker's own words, 2026-11-17)
- **Two tracks, on purpose.** Professional practice (DD, CDs, CA, millwork) is
  the apprenticeship he is doing now. Research and authorship are the destination.
  He is in practice until his research and name can stand on their own.

## Working method
- Starts from a question, not a program. Radical questioning drives the work.
  (Still need examples of the actual questions.)
- **Between continuity and transformation.** The inherited condition (an
  existing building, a brand, a city) is a productive constraint. Keep what
  ages with dignity, isolate it, and present it as an artefact.
- **Allusion over quotation.** "A curve may recall a classical arch without
  becoming one."
- **The threshold or boundary is where the design happens.** It runs from the
  thesis facades to La Rustica's thresholds chapter.
- **Thinks in pairs:** rustic/pristine, old/new, opaque/translucent.

## Concept deck method (his standard, from solo work)
Cover with project number → a witty epigraph page → Introduction / Premise /
Content → I Context → II Proposal abstract (a 3–4 word principle set) →
III User or Origin → Design narrative (Aesthetics, Materials, Lighting,
Furniture, Sketches) → Summary → Glossary → Copyright.

## How his mind moves (from the research handoffs)
- **Reaches for the hardest frame.** Black holes, algebraic topology, quantum
  error correction. He takes ideas from the far edge of other fields and asks
  what they mean for space.
- **Speculates, then disciplines himself.** He wants wild ideas held to rigor:
  facts, analogies and conjectures kept apart. Ambition and self-skepticism at once.
- **Thinks in dimensions.** 2D ↔ 3D, boundary ↔ volume, appearance ↔ information.
  This is the same pair-thinking, at the level of ideas.
- **Distrusts the visible.** "Visuality is merely a consequence of informational
  flow." What looks the same may not be the same; what matters may be unseen.
- **Uses AI as a sparring partner** to stress-test ideas, not to produce them.

## The engine (raw grad-school draft, 2025–26)
- Driven by interior friction and relentless self-critique, not by good intentions:
  "I'm not curious, I'm epistemically compulsed."
- Conditions handed to him, his response chosen: agency, not victimhood. He refuses
  identity performance or adversity as the story; judge and present him by his ideas.
- Truth over comfort: "not should this be known, but can this be known."
- Allergic to performance, including performed honesty. Honest critique is what he
  wants from Cornelius, not reassurance.
- Personal background stays private (see source/writings notes). Never use it in
  client-facing work or as a narrative device.

## Design intelligence (grad-school writings, 2024–26)
- "Architecture should encode intelligence before it computes it." Computation
  executes and propagates intelligence; it must not be mistaken for it.
- Goal: encode a designer's hidden structure (hierarchies, dependencies, embedded
  expression) into relational systems without flattening the sensibility, so that
  "the intelligence of a designer becomes transmissible."
- Problem-setting over problem-solving (Donald Schön): redefine the problem first.
- His method, in his words: see "the distance between how a system represents
  reality and what reality actually is."
- Open tension: architectural autonomy ("geometrical first") vs "architecture is
  rarely autonomous." Unresolved; don't resolve it for him.
- Practice wasn't idle: at Lissoni he built relational Grasshopper/Python systems
  for millwork, lighting and material.

## Research thesis (from his research handoffs, 2026)
- Architecture as relationships, not geometry: a lower-dimensional boundary
  can encode a higher-dimensional space; identity is informational, not visual.
- His line: "visuality is merely a consequence of informational flow, the
  product in which we enjoy."
- Paradox to keep alive: an image-maker who argues the image is a byproduct.
- He rejects "architectural romanticism and fetishized computation", and
  separates fact, analogy and conjecture. Hold him to this.
