# Aesthetic

Evidence weight (set by the maker, 2026-11-17):
1. **His own work, all at equal weight:**
   - Research work: Input/Output Correspondence, The Imaginary Variance, XATA,
     Deconstruction Underway. His collaborators, Halie Kim and Elizabeth Bobyr,
     share his mentality, so this work reads as fully his.
   - Academic work: Unwinding Reveries, Palindrome, Eleutheromania, Cinematic
     Rhythms, Binary Library.
   - Solo concept decks: Al-Izdihar Villa, La Rustica.
2. **Firm work** (EB2 / Lissoni). It reflects the firm's voice, not his.
   Use it only for craft and execution, never for voice or taste.

## Drawing and image-making
- **Grey given, colored graft.** The existing condition is drawn as a grey line
  ghost; the intervention arrives as saturated collage. (Imaginary Variance)
- **Metaphysical collage stage.** An infinite white perspective grid floor,
  cut-out furniture, classical statues, line-drawn people over photo collage.
  (Al-Izdihar and La Rustica sketches)
- **Quiet surrealism.** Recurring props: the moon, flocks of birds, the
  reflective sphere, arches, a lone tree on rock. (Cover, Palindrome,
  Eleutheromania, concept epigraphs)
- **Catalogs and taxonomies:** grids of fragments, typologies, process strips.

## Interior palette rule (solo work)
- A warm, dark base plus ONE cool counterpoint.
  Villa: dark timber, plaster, limestone + green marble.
  La Rustica: brick, wood + blue-grey plaster.
- Textured and reeded glass, glass block: translucency over transparency.

## Light
- Indirect, diffused, glowing; the source hidden; "cinematic". No exposed
  downlights. Light is a material.

## Objects and furniture
- Collected, not decorated. Italian mid-century and modernism, sculptural
  pieces, vintage objects with patina. Each piece gets room to be read.
- Inherited objects are isolated and re-presented as artefacts.

## Graphic design
- ✓ **Helvetica / Helvetica Neue only.** His current favourite. Grotesque sans; bold caps titles;
  titles end in a period (CV, portfolio). (Maker, 2026-11-17)
- ✓ **Never a serif.** Not for epigraphs, captions or labels. (Maker, 2026-11-17)
- ✓ **No red in his text.** Red is his firm's (Alareen) colour, not his. Use it only when the
  firm's branding requires it, and never let it read as his signature. Telling him apart
  from the firm is a priority. (Maker, 2026-11-17)
- Concept decks: big project number on the cover, Roman-numeral chapters,
  generous white space, image clusters, small running footer.

- ✓ **Black pages rarely, and only for a specific reason.** Decks default to white; the warm-dark
  palette rule is for interiors, not page backgrounds. (Maker, 2026-11-17)
- ✓ **Image quality is a gate, not a given.** Every image in a deck must stand at the level of his
  references. A mediocre render next to sophisticated references cheapens all of them. Leave it
  out rather than use it because it exists. (Maker, 2026-11-17, on the P-1062 renders)
