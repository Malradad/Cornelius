# Voice

## Confirmed by the maker
- **Two registers, one of them is his.** The research and project writing is his
  voice. The professional (Lissoni) writing reads like a hotel brochure, and he
  agrees. Never imitate the brochure register. (Maker, 2026-11-17)

## His register (from research work)
- Opens with questions: "What narratives can emerge through the defamiliarization
  of formal fragments…?" (Imaginary Variance)
- Manifesto verbs: challenge, counteract, disrupt, graft, hack, reassemble.
- Titles are the purest voice: strange, erudite, single words or short phrases,
  ending in a period. Eleutheromania. Palindrome. The Imaginary Variance.
- Weakness to correct: endings that fall back on reassurance ("more inclusive,
  equitable…"). End on the question or the provocation instead.

## His private register (grad-school notes, 2025–26)
- Aphoristic and adversarial: "I'm not curious, I'm epistemically compulsed."
  "I would rather be legible than liked." "Architecture should encode intelligence
  before it computes it."
- Formula sentences that compress a whole argument: "spectacle is the problem,
  pragmatism is the pressure, information structure is the answer, automation is
  the execution mechanism."
- His self-critique is his sharpest writing. When drafting for him, write like his
  notes, not like his applications.
- The application register ("push the boundaries", "the perfect place", "new
  frontiers") is banned, like the brochure register.

## Banned (the brochure register)
luxurious, immersive, sophisticated, harmonious, meticulously, seamless,
"elevates the experience", "timeless", "every detail".
